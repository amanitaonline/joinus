Browsers block most cross-site requests. If a direct fetch fails, the page is retried through public CORS proxies (the URL is then sent to those third parties). For sites that still refuse, open the page, "View source", and use the Paste tab. Photo previews load straight from the site, so they usually show even when fetching is blocked. If Download is blocked, use Open and save from the new tab.
